# Design Review Checklist

Mark each item Pass, Fail, or N/A. Fix every Fail before delivery.

## Direction
- [ ] Audience and main action are stated for each screen.
- [ ] Visual direction is consistent with the brand brief.
- [ ] Nothing looks like a default template or generated filler.

## Hierarchy
- [ ] One clear focal point per view.
- [ ] Headings, body, and secondary text are distinguishable at a glance.
- [ ] Primary action is the most visually prominent control.

## Spacing and alignment
- [ ] All spacing values come from the scale.
- [ ] Left edges align within each column.
- [ ] Related items are closer than unrelated ones.
- [ ] Equal-height cards share internal structure.

## Typography
- [ ] No more than two type families.
- [ ] Type sizes come from the named scale.
- [ ] Body text is at least 16 px on mobile; line lengths are readable.

## Color
- [ ] Colors are referenced by role tokens.
- [ ] Text contrast meets 4.5:1 (body) and 3:1 (large text, UI boundaries).
- [ ] Accent use is restrained and purposeful.

## Surfaces
- [ ] Radii follow the scale; nested radii are smaller than parents.
- [ ] Shadows come from the scale; no decorative stacking.
- [ ] Borders are consistent in weight and color.

## Icons and images
- [ ] One icon set, one stroke width, consistent sizes.
- [ ] Images have fixed aspect ratios and appropriate crops.

## States
- [ ] Default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, empty, error, success are designed for each interactive element.
- [ ] Empty and error states give a next action.

## Motion
- [ ] Motion has a purpose and stays brief.
- [ ] Reduced-motion preference is respected.

## Copy
- [ ] Labels are specific; no placeholder text remains.
- [ ] Error and success messages say what happened and what to do.
